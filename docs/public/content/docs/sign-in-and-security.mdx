## Your account

Your own account lives in **Profile**. Click the round button with your
initial at the top right and choose **Profile**. There you can:

- **Change password**. Your other sessions are signed out.
- **Set up authenticator app**: scan the QR code with an app such as a
  password manager or an authenticator app, and enter a 6-digit code.
- **Add passkey**: sign in with your fingerprint, face or a security key
  instead of a code.
- **Generate new codes**: 10 recovery codes, each works once. Keep them
  somewhere safe. Use one when you can't use your authenticator app.

The **API tokens** tab next to it holds your
[API tokens](/docs/api-tokens).

Passwords need at least 15 characters. Long passphrases are welcome.
Common and leaked passwords are refused.

## Signing in

After your password, Docker Manager asks for your authenticator code or
passkey, if you set one up. Lost them? Choose **Use a recovery code**, or
ask the owner to reset your sign-in factors.

Forgot your password? Ask the owner for a reset link.

You are signed out after 1 hour without activity, and after 24 hours at
the latest.

For sensitive actions, such as creating an API token, Docker Manager asks
you to **Confirm it's you** again. That lasts 10 minutes.

<Callout title="Passkeys belong to one address">
  If Docker Manager moves to another address, existing passkeys stop
  working. Sign in with your password and authenticator code, and add the
  passkey again.
</Callout>

## The sign-in policy

The owner sets the rules for everyone under **Settings → Sign-in policy**:

- **Strict passwords** and the **Minimum length**.
- What every account signs in with, for example **Password and
  authenticator app**, or **Passkey**.
- **Time to add required factors**: when you require a new factor, people
  get this long to add it (72 hours by default). After that, only the owner
  can reset their account.
- How long invitation and password reset links work.
- Whether **API tokens** are allowed, and their longest lifetime (90 days
  by default).

Changing the required factors signs everyone out.

## Locked out of the owner account

On the manager's server, in the folder with your `compose.yaml`, run:

```bash
docker compose exec docker-manager docker-manager owner-recovery
```

It prints a code and a link that work once, for one hour, and signs the
owner out everywhere. Open the link and set a new password. Your
authenticator app, passkeys and recovery codes are removed; set them up
again after you sign in.
