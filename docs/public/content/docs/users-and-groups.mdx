---
title: Users & Groups
description: Invite people, organize them in groups and keep sign-ins safe.
---

The person who set up Docker Manager is its **owner**. The owner can do
everything and is the only one who manages people, under **Access** in the
menu. Nobody can sign up on their own.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Access** and press **Invite User**.
2. Press **Create Invite Link**, copy the link and send it yourself.
   Docker Manager doesn't email it.

The link works once and expires after 72 hours, unless you choose another
time under **Options**. The person opens it,
chooses a username and password, and presses **Create Account**. The
**Invitations** tab lists your links; **Revoke** stops an unused one.

## Groups

A person can be in several **groups**, and the groups'
[permissions](/permissions) decide what they may do. New accounts join
the group marked **Default for New Users**: at first **Restricted**, which
gives no access.

- **Create Group** makes a new group. It starts at the bottom of the list.
- Drag a group by its handle to change its priority. Groups higher in
  the list win: for someone in several groups, the highest group with a
  rule for an action decides.
- **Add Members** on a group's page adds people to it; the remove button
  next to a member takes them out of that group only.
- A group's menu can **Rename Group**, **Make Default for New Users** or
  **Delete Group** (only an empty group that is not the default).

## Manage a person

Open a person under **Access** to see their **Groups** (in priority
order), **Add to Group** or remove them from one, see their **Sessions**,
or use **Account Actions**:

- **Disable Account** signs them out and revokes their API tokens.
  **Enable Account** lets them back in.
- **Sign Out Everywhere**.
- **Reset Sign-In Factors** removes their authenticator app, passkeys and
  recovery codes.
- **Create Password Reset Link** makes a one-time link to send them.
- **Delete Account**.

The owner account can't be disabled or deleted.

## Your account

Click the round button with your initial at the top right and choose
**Profile**:

- **Change Password**. Passwords need at least 15 characters, unless the
  owner changed this in the sign-in policy.
- **Set Up Authenticator App** to enter a code after your password, or
  **Add Passkey** to sign in with your fingerprint, face or PIN instead of
  a password.
- **Generate New Codes**: 10 recovery codes for when you can't use your
  authenticator app.
- **Sessions** lists the devices you are signed in on. **Sign Out** next to
  one signs it out at once.

Some changes, such as creating an API token or changing the sign-in
policy, first ask you to **Confirm It's You** with one method:

- With a passkey, your browser asks for it right away.
- Otherwise, with an authenticator app, enter its code.
- Otherwise, enter your password.

To use another method, choose **Use Authenticator Code Instead**, **Use a
Passkey Instead** or **Use Your Password Instead**. Your browser
remembers whether you prefer the passkey or the code, also for signing
in. A confirmation lasts 10 minutes.

You are signed out after 8 hours without activity and after 24 hours at
most. On your own computer, tick **Stay Signed In** to stay signed in for
up to a year. Passkeys belong to one address: after Docker Manager moves to
a new address, add them again.

## Sign-in policy

The owner sets the rules for everyone under **Settings → Sign-In Policy**:
password rules, which sign-in factors every account must use, how long
links work, whether API tokens are allowed, and whether **Stay Signed In**
is allowed. Changing the required factors signs everyone else out.
