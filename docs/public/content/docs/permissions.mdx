---
title: Permissions
description: Decide what each group and person may see and do.
---

Permissions are rules on a group: for each action, **Allow** or **Deny**.
**No Rule** leaves the action to a broader rule, such as one for the whole
environment; without any rule, it is denied. So a new account can't do
anything until you add it to a group or give it overrides. The owner can always do
everything and is the only one who manages permissions.

## Give permissions

1. Open **Access → Groups**, then a group.
2. In the tree on the left, pick where the rules apply:
   - **All Resources**: everything, including environments you add later,
   - one environment, with everything on it,
   - or one stack, stack service, container, volume, template and so on.
3. Pick a preset under **Start From**, or set single actions. Each action
   names what it acts on, such as **Restart Containers**. Risky actions
   are marked **High Risk**; rarely needed ones are folded away at the
   end of each section.
4. Press **Save Permissions** and confirm the list of changes.

The presets:

- **Viewer**: sees everything, changes nothing. Logs, files and secrets
  stay hidden.
- **Operator**: also deploys, starts, stops, restarts and recreates, pulls
  images, checks for image updates, runs backups, reads logs and dismisses
  alerts. Stopping a stack removes its containers but keeps its data;
  nothing else is deleted, and no settings change.
- **Admin**: can do everything in that place, except what only the owner
  can do.

Within a group, the most specific rule decides: a rule on one stack wins
over its environment, which wins over **All Resources**.

**Start Stacks**, **Stop Stacks** and **Restart Stacks** can also be given
on one service of a stack, under the stack in the tree. Starting a service
also starts the services it depends on, so the rule must cover those too.

For someone in several groups, the groups are checked from the top of the
**Groups** list: the first group with a rule for the action decides, even
if a lower group has a more specific rule. Drag the groups to change their
order.

## Rules for one person

Open a person under **Access** to set **Overrides**: rules for just them,
which win over the rules of all their groups. **Effective Access** shows what they
may do in the end. **View as This User** checks their access again; with
unsaved overrides, the button reads **Preview with Unsaved Changes**.

## What only the owner can do

Some things can't be given to anyone else: managing people, groups and the
sign-in policy, other people's API tokens, registry connections and Git
credentials, notification channels, template sources, backing up,
restoring and moving Docker Manager itself, and the support bundle. Rules
never apply to the owner.
