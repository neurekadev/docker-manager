---
title: Terminal
description: Open a shell inside a container from your browser.
---

Open the **Terminal** tab of a stack or a container. Pick the **Container**
(on a stack) and the **Shell**, and press **Connect**. The terminal icon in
a service's row on the stack's **Overview** opens one quickly.

**Detect Automatically** uses Bash if the container has it, otherwise sh.
You can also choose Bash, sh or Zsh.

Good to know:

- A terminal only works in a running container that has a shell. It never
  opens a shell on the server itself.
- You can have up to 4 terminals open at once.
- A terminal closes after 30 minutes without typing or output (you get a
  warning at 25), and after 8 hours in total. Press **Connect Again** to
  start a new one.
