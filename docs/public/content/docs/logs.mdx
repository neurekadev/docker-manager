---
title: Logs
description: Read what your containers write, live.
---

Open the **Logs** tab of a stack or a container. New lines appear as they
are written.

- **Search logs** highlights matching lines. **Matching lines only** hides
  the rest.
- **Errors only** shows only what the containers wrote as errors.
- **Wrap lines**, **Timestamps** and **Follow** change how lines are shown.
  Turn off **Follow** to stop scrolling while you read.
- **Download shown lines** saves what you see to a file.
- **Open in a new window** opens the logs in their own window.

On a stack, pick the services to show, or **All services**. **Logs of** in a
service's row menu opens the logs of just that service.

The view starts with the last 200 lines of each container and keeps the
newest 5,000.
