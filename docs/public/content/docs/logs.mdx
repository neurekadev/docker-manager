---
title: Logs
description: Read what your containers write, live.
---

Open the **Logs** tab of a stack or a container. New lines appear as they
are written.

- **Search logs** shows only the lines that match as you type, with the
  matches highlighted. **Match case** and **Use regular expression** change
  how the search reads your text. Press Escape to clear it.
- **Levels** picks which lines to show: **Error**, **Warning**, **Info**,
  **Debug**, **Verbose** and **Other** (lines without a level), and under
  **Output** the containers' **Standard output** and **Standard error**.
  Each shows how many lines it has. **Only** keeps just that one,
  **Select all** shows everything again.
- **Wrap lines**, **Timestamps** and **Follow** change how lines are shown.
  Turn off **Follow** to stop scrolling while you read.
- **Download shown lines** saves what you see to a file.
- **Open in a new window** opens the logs in their own window.

On a stack, **Services** picks which services to show and how many lines
each has. **Only** keeps just that one, **Select all** shows every service
again. **Logs of** in a
service's row menu opens the logs of just that service.

## Good to know

- The level comes from the line's text, in the usual formats such as
  `level=warn`, `"level":"error"`, `[info]` or `ERROR`. Indented lines,
  such as the lines of a stack trace, keep the level of the line above.
- Error lines have a red edge and warnings an amber one.
- The view starts with the last 200 lines of each container and keeps the
  newest 5,000.
