## Logs

Open the **Logs** tab of a stack or a container. New lines appear as they
are written.

- **Search logs** highlights matching lines. Turn on **Matching lines only**
  to hide the rest.
- **Errors only** shows only what the containers wrote as errors (their
  error output).
- **Wrap lines**, **Timestamps** and **Follow** change how lines are shown.
  Turn off **Follow** to stop scrolling while you read.
- **Download shown lines** saves the lines you see, with your filters, to a
  file.
- **Open in a new window** opens the logs in their own window, handy next
  to other work.

On a stack, pick the services to show at the top, or choose **All
services**. **Logs of** in a service's row menu opens the logs of just that
service.

The view starts with the last 200 lines and keeps the newest 5,000.

## Terminals

Open the **Terminal** tab of a stack or a container, pick the **Container**
(on a stack) and the **Shell**, and press **Connect**.

- **Detect automatically** picks the best shell the container has. You can
  also choose Bash, sh or Zsh.
- A terminal only works in a running container that has a shell. It never
  opens a shell on the server itself.
- You can have up to 4 terminals open at once.
- After 25 minutes without any typing or output you get a warning, and
  the terminal closes at 30 minutes. It also closes after 8 hours in total.
  Press **Connect again** to start a new one.

To open a terminal quickly, use the terminal icon in a service's row on the
stack's **Overview**.
