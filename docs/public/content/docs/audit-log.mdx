---
title: Audit Log
description: See who did what, when and from where.
---

**Settings → Audit log** records every sign-in, permission change and
action: who did it, when, from where and the result. Nobody can change or
delete a record.

- Filter by **Who**, **Outcome**, **Category** and **When**. **More
  filters** adds environments, dates, actions and resources.
- Open a record to see its details and **What changed**.
- **Export CSV** and **Export NDJSON** download the records that match your
  filters.

The owner can let others read it with the **View audit log** and **Export
audit log** permissions. A stack's **Activity** tab shows its jobs and,
for people who may read the audit log, its records.

Records are kept for 365 days, and at most 1 GB of them. To change this,
see [Configuration](/docs/configuration#manager-settings).
