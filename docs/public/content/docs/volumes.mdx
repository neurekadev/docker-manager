---
title: Volumes
description: Create volumes, browse their files, back them up and move them.
---

**Volumes** lists your volumes, with filters for driver, stack, unused and
managed volumes.

- **Create volume** makes a new volume.
- **Files** opens the volume's data in the [File Manager](/docs/file-manager).
- **Backups** lists its backups and restores them (see
  [Backups](/docs/backups)).
- **Migrate** copies it to another server (see
  [Migrations](/docs/migrations#move-a-volume)).
- **Remove** deletes the volume **and its data**. Type its name to confirm.
  A volume in use, or one of a stack, can't be removed here.

Only `local` volumes on the server's own disk can be browsed and backed up.
Others, including NFS or CIFS volumes, show as **Read-only**. Docker
Manager's own volumes can't be browsed, backed up, migrated or removed.

To remove several volumes, tick their rows and press **Remove**. You see
what will be removed and what is left out before you confirm. **Prune
volumes** removes unused ones (see [Maintenance](/docs/maintenance)).
