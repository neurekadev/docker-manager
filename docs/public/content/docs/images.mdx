---
title: Images
description: Pull, tag and remove images on your servers.
---

**Images** lists the images on your servers, which containers use them,
their size and when they were built. Tick **Unused** to see only images no
container uses.

- **Pull image** downloads an image. Enter a name such as `nginx:1.27`;
  without a tag, `latest` is pulled. The dialog shows which
  [registry login](/registries) it will use.
- **Tag** gives an image another name.
- **Create container** opens the [container form](/containers#create-a-container)
  with this image filled in.
- **Remove** asks you to type the image's name. An image that containers
  still use can't be removed.

Images without a tag are listed at the end. **Prune images** removes unused
ones (see [Maintenance](/maintenance)).

To remove several images, tick their rows and press **Remove**. You see
what will be removed and what is left out before you confirm.

To build your own images, see [Builds](/builds). To keep images up to
date, see [Updates](/updates).
